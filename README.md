#Vrinda Store Annual Sales Dashboard Using Excel

Project Overview

This project presents an interactive retail sales dashboard created in Microsoft Excel. It converts raw order data into clear business insights using PivotTables, PivotCharts, slicers, and KPI-focused visualizations.

The dashboard helps decision-makers quickly understand sales performance, customer demographics, order status, sales channels, and the best-performing states. It demonstrates practical skills in data cleaning, analysis, visualization, and business reporting.

Dashboard Preview

Business Objective

The main objective is to analyze annual retail sales data and answer important business questions:

How do monthly sales and order volumes change over time?

Which customer gender and age group contributes the most orders?

Which states generate the highest sales?

Which online sales channels perform best?

What percentage of orders are delivered, returned, cancelled, or refunded?

How can these insights support better marketing and sales decisions?

Dashboard Highlights

Monthly comparison of total sales and order volume

Sales contribution by men and women

Order distribution by gender and age group

Top five states by total sales

Order distribution across Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Meesho, Ajio, Nalli, and other channels

Order-status analysis for delivered, returned, cancelled, and refunded orders

Interactive slicers for month, channel, and product category

Key Insights

Based on the dashboard shown in this repository:

Women contribute approximately 62% of sales, compared with 38% from men.

The adult women segment records the highest share of orders at approximately 34.59%.

Approximately 92% of orders are delivered, indicating a strong fulfilment rate.

Amazon is the leading sales channel with approximately 35% of orders.

Myntra and Flipkart follow with approximately 23% and 22%, respectively.

Maharashtra is the top-performing state, followed by Karnataka and Uttar Pradesh.

The strongest customer group is adult women, making it an important target segment for future campaigns.

Business Recommendations

Focus advertising and promotional campaigns on women, especially the adult customer segment.

Strengthen campaigns on Amazon, Myntra, and Flipkart because they generate most online orders.

Prioritize high-performing states such as Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh for targeted offers.

Investigate returned and cancelled orders to improve customer satisfaction and reduce revenue loss.

Use monthly sales patterns to plan inventory, discounts, and marketing activities.

Interpreted the results and prepared actionable business recommendations.
